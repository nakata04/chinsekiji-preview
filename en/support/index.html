Support the Temple

About Donations

Our temple continues its work to maintain the temple, preserve its treasures and cultural properties, care for its grounds, and share its history with future generations.

We accept voluntary donations from those who support these activities.

Donations are entirely voluntary. The amount of a donation does not change how visitors are received or treated by the temple.

Intended Use

  • Leave it to the temple
  • Temple maintenance
  • Temple treasures & cultural properties
  • Grounds maintenance
  • Community outreach

Your preference will be taken into account when the funds are used. It is not a guarantee that funds will be used for a specific purpose.

Online donations are currently unavailable.

For questions or urgent requests, please call us or get in touch.
