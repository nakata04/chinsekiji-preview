Frequently Asked Questions

Can anyone visit Chinsekiji Temple?

Anyone who wishes to visit quietly is welcome. However, entering the Main Hall, viewing the temple treasures, and taking part during memorial services and events may vary depending on the time and circumstances. If you have questions, please contact the temple in advance.

Can visitors see the temple's treasures?

Some temple treasures and cultural properties are not on permanent public display. Please check our latest news for special viewings.

Can I ask about Buddhist memorial services?

Yes. Details are provided individually, so please contact the temple.

Is photography allowed?

Please follow the guidance posted on site about where and when photography is allowed. Restrictions may apply, for example during special viewings of cultural properties or during memorial services and events.
